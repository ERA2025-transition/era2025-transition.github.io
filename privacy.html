This website and the Travel Adventures by Eunice brand are operated by Rosado Management Solutions Inc. (“we,” “our,” or “us”). We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect across our website, email sign-ups, and social media channels, how we use it, and the choices and rights you have.

Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you sign up for a free resource, guide, checklist, newsletter, or offer, we collect the email address you submit and any other details you choose to provide.

Information collected automatically. When you visit our website we may collect basic, non-identifying analytics such as pages viewed and general device or browser type, using cookies or similar technologies.

Social media integrations. When we connect our own social media accounts (such as TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube) to third-party analytics tools like Composio, we access only our own account’s profile information and public performance statistics for our internal analytics. We do not collect personal data about other users through these integrations.

How We Use Your Information

  • To send you the free resource or content you requested.
  • To send occasional marketing emails — travel tips, new content, and offers — where you have given consent.
  • To measure and improve our content and website.

For visitors in the EEA and UK, our legal basis for sending marketing emails is your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

Email Marketing & Your Choices

We add you to our email list only with your consent, and we use a double opt-in process to confirm it. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time; we honor unsubscribe requests promptly. We use Brevo (Sendinblue) as our email service provider to store contacts and deliver messages on our behalf.

Who We Share Data With

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us operate — such as Brevo for email delivery and Composio for analytics — and only as needed to provide these services.

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as you remain subscribed or until you ask us to delete it, after which we remove it from our active lists.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and various US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, and to withdraw consent or object to its processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address below.

Cookies

Our site may use cookies for basic functionality and analytics. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

International Users

We are based in the United States. If you contact us or subscribe from outside the US, your information will be processed in the US.

Children

Our content is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
