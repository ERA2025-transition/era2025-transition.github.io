The Spain Road Trip Starter

Pick the one that sounds like you, not the one that sounds impressive.

ROUTE 01

Andalucía Classic

Málaga → Ronda → Seville → Córdoba → Granada

The one everybody means when they say Spain. White villages on cliffs, the Alhambra at golden hour, orange trees in the middle of the city.

ROUTE 02

Green Spain

San Sebastián → Bilbao → Asturias → Galicia

The Spain nobody posts. Cliffs, fishing villages, and the best food in the country. Cool in August when the south is an oven.

ROUTE 03

Catalonia and the Costa Brava

Barcelona → Girona → Cadaqués → Empordà

The least driving of any route here. Coves you walk down to, a Dalí house on the water, medieval villages inland.

ROUTE 04

The Castilian Heartland

Madrid → Segovia → Salamanca → Toledo

Castles, cathedrals, and a Roman aqueduct holding itself up with no mortar. The cheapest route on this list by a wide margin.

ROUTE 05

Valencia to Alicante

Valencia → Xàtiva → Altea → Alicante

Paella where it was invented, blue domes over the sea, and the gentlest driving in Spain.

🔴 THE HACK

The €200 mistake

Every Spanish city over 50,000 people now has a low emission zone. Drive in without clearance and the fine is €200, plus whatever your rental company adds for passing it on. Per entry. No tourist exemption.

Your rental car is almost certainly fine, and there are ten seconds of work at the desk that make sure of it. The full guide has the exact ask, the one city that makes you pre-register, and the parking rule that saves you from reversing down a donkey street in front of an audience.

Plus the number most people get wrong: the coastal AP-7 has been free since 2021. That drive costs you nothing but fuel.
