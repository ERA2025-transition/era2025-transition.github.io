Chapter 1

When to go

Timing changes everything — the crowds, the prices, even the wind.

✅ The sweet spot — May–June & September–October

Warm sea, long sunny days, dreamy light, and far smaller crowds and lower prices than peak. Come now for that red-dress shot with room to breathe.

☀️ Peak — July & August

Hottest, busiest, priciest, with electric nightlife — book far ahead. August brings the meltemi wind: great for a flying dress, tricky for ferries.

🍂 Quiet — November–April

Cheap and calm, but many hotels, beach clubs and restaurants close. Not ideal for a first trip.

Eunice's take: Aim for late May or September — best of everything, and the golden light does half the work in your photos.
Chapter 2

Getting there

✈️ Fly

Mykonos Airport (JMK) has direct seasonal flights from major European cities in summer, plus quick hops from Athens (ATH) year-round. Fastest option — book early, fares climb fast in peak.

⛴️ Ferry

From Athens, ferries leave from Piraeus and Rafina. High-speed boats run roughly 2.5–4.5 hours. Scenic and often cheaper — build in buffer time on windy August days.

Smart move: Landing in Athens? Make it two trips in one — a night in Athens, then island time. Grab a travel eSIM before you fly so maps and rideshares work the second you land.
Chapter 3

Where to stay

Mykonos Town (Chora)

Whitewashed maze, windmills, boutiques, nightlife. Central and iconic — best for first-timers.

Ornos

Calm, sandy, family-friendly beach with easy access to town. Great home base.

Platis Gialos & Psarou

Beach-club energy, glamorous, walkable to restaurants. Where to be seen.

Agios Ioannis

Quieter, with stunning sunsets over the sea. Romantic and photogenic.

Chapter 4 — the star of the show

The flying-dress photoshoot

That red-dress shot against the white church? That's a flying dress photoshoot — a signature Greek-islands experience, and more doable than it looks.

  • You book a session; the studio provides the dramatic long dress (red is unbeatable against the white walls).
  • A photographer poses you at iconic spots and throws the fabric so it "flies" — no Photoshop, just timing and fearlessness.
  • Sessions run early morning or golden hour for the best light and fewest crowds. You get a gallery of edited shots.
Eunice's tips: Wear nude strapless underthings, bring heels you can walk in, keep makeup light, and commit to the pose — confidence is the whole shot. Book golden hour, and book ahead; slots fill.
