The 9-to-5 Escape Checklist

1. Pick the skill that pays from anywhere.

Name one skill someone already pays you for, or would.

Check it travels: laptop, your own hours.

Get one paying proof, even a small one.

2. Build the runway.

Add up six months of real expenses. That's your number.

Open a separate escape fund. Automate a weekly transfer.

Cut one recurring cost this week, send it to the fund.

3. Start the side income before you jump.

Land your first client or sale while still employed.

Repeat it three times. Three is a pattern, not luck.

Track the monthly number as it climbs toward your bills.

4. Plan the clean exit.

Put a real leave date on the calendar.

Give notice like a pro. No burned bridges.

Line up your first 30 days: where, what, who.
